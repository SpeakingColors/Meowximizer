# Intro
Meowximizer is a sonic enhancer, bass enhancer and exciter in one. It splits the sound into three phase-aligned bands and works on each: bass contour and harmonics, mid and high timing and dynamic lift, an air exciter, a squeeze section (compression, push and opto-style smoothing with several styles), warm saturation and an output limiter. Aim: put it on a lead or bass and it gets thicker, smoother and louder without losing punch.

# Quick start
- Pick a preset close to your source (Thick Supersaw, Dance Master Lead, Mix Bus Glue, Kick Beef).
- Choose a Squeeze style in the menu: it sets the Squeeze, Push and Opto knobs and changes how the chain works.
- Adjust Tone (bass, process, air) by ear, then Warm for saturation.
- Set Ceiling for the limiter and match Output to the bypassed level.

# Recommendations
- Leads: Thick Supersaw or Dance Master styles, Opto 50 to 85, then lower Mix if it sounds crowded.
- Bass: Tight Lock or Smooth styles, keep Bass harmonics moderate, check mono and small speakers.
- Mix bus or master: Glue or Gentle style, low Squeeze, Ceiling at -1 dB.
- Hardstyle kicks: Hardstyle Lock style with Push 1 and a Ceiling that leaves room for your mastering chain.

# New in this version
- Latency: the plugin reports its exact latency to the host (about 3.4 ms at 48 kHz: 2x oversampling stages plus a 1.5 ms limiter lookahead). The host compensates automatically.
- Clip knob and Clipper position: a smooth peak clipper (up to 9 dB harder) before the limiter, or on the input.
- Warm character: plain tanh, Drive, Transistor, Fuzz, or Stack.
- Air exciter: tanh or the PurrOne stack (gritty when pushed). Air also adds a smooth 9 kHz shelf.
- Loudness match (Auto, Off, On): lifts the processed sound back to the input level, never past the Ceiling.
- The Ceiling holds exactly: the limiter is a lookahead limiter with no overshoot.
- Every level-type knob is smoothed over 20 ms at most, so there is no zipper noise and no delay when you move a knob.
